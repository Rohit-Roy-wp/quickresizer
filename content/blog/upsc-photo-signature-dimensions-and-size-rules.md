# UPSC Civil Services Photo & Signature Rules: Dimensions, Size, and Date Stamp

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducts prestigious examinations including the Civil Services Examination (CSE - IAS, IPS, IFS), NDA, CDS, CMS, and Indian Forest Service. Through its One-Time Registration (OTR) portal (upsconline.nic.in), UPSC mandates specific square image dimensions and file size ceilings.

In recent notifications, UPSC introduced stricter photographic requirements regarding candidate appearance and freshness. Here is what every aspirant must know.

---

## 1. UPSC Official Dimensions and File Size Rules

- **Aspect Ratio:** Strictly square (1:1 aspect ratio).
- **Minimum Dimensions:** **350 &times; 350 pixels**.
- **Maximum Dimensions:** 1000 &times; 1000 pixels.
- **File Size Window:** Strictly between **20 KB and 300 KB**.
- **File Format:** JPG / JPEG format only.

---

## 2. The &quot;Three-Quarter&quot; Facial Coverage Mandate

UPSC requires that the candidate&apos;s face occupy **at least three-fourths (75% to 80%)** of the overall photograph space. Full-body shots, landscape portraits, or pictures where the applicant appears far from the camera will trigger automated application disqualification.

---

## 3. Freshness & Date Stamp Requirement

Under recent UPSC guidelines:

- The photograph must not be more than **10 days old** from the date of commencement of the application process.
- The name of the candidate and the date on which the photograph was taken must be clearly inscribed at the bottom of the photograph if mandated by the specific year&apos;s notification.
- The candidate&apos;s appearance (beard, hairstyle, glasses) on examination day must match the uploaded photograph.
