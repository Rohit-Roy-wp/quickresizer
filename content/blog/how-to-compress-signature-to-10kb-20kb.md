# How to Compress Scanned Signature to 10KB - 20KB for Exam Applications

Nearly every government recruitment portal, banking exam (IBPS/SBI), and university entrance form requires candidates to upload a digital scan of their signature within a tight 10 KB to 20 KB limit.

Because smartphone camera photos of paper signatures often exceed 2 MB to 5 MB, compressing them down to 15 KB while keeping ink strokes sharp and legible is a common headache. This tutorial outlines how to prepare and compress your signature scan cleanly.

---

## 1. Golden Rules for Capturing Your Signature

Before compressing your image, capturing a high-contrast original is 90% of the battle:

- **Paper Quality:** Use pure white unruled printer paper (A4 standard). Never use lined notebook paper or recycled gray paper.
- **Ink Choice:** Use a dark black or deep blue gel or ballpoint pen. Do not use sketch pens or fountain pens with ink bleeding.
- **Lighting:** Take the photo in bright daylight near a window or under a direct overhead lamp to avoid phone shadows across the paper.
- **Crop First:** Before uploading, crop the camera image tightly around the signature, leaving about 5mm of white margin around the text.

---

## 2. Standard Official Signature Dimensions

- **SSC Standard:** 4.0 cm (width) &times; 2.0 cm (height), 10 KB – 20 KB.
- **IBPS Standard:** 140 pixels (width) &times; 60 pixels (height), 10 KB – 20 KB.
- **Format:** JPEG / JPG only.

---

## 3. The Black Background Trap (and How to Avoid It)

A frequent bug on free online image converters occurs when converting transparent PNGs or scans to JPEG: without white-fill compositing, transparency turns solid black, ruining the signature.

QuickResizer automatically pre-fills every JPEG canvas with pure solid white (`#ffffff`). When you select the **Pad White** fit mode, your signature text remains crisp against a spotless white background.

---

## 4. Compressing to 10 KB - 20 KB with QuickResizer

1. Upload your cropped signature photo into QuickResizer.
2. Select the **SSC Signature** or **IBPS Signature** preset.
3. Verify that Width is 4.0 cm (or 140 px) and Height is 2.0 cm (or 60 px).
4. Set Minimum KB to 10 and Maximum KB to 20.
5. Click **Resize & Compress**.
6. Download your optimized file and check that the size is between 12 KB and 19 KB.
