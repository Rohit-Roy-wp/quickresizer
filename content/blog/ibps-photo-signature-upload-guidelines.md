# IBPS PO & Clerk Photo and Signature Upload Guidelines (2026 Updated)

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) oversees recruitment for Probationary Officers (PO), Clerical Cadre, Specialist Officers (SO), and Regional Rural Banks (RRB). The IBPS online registration system is notorious for strict upload requirements covering four distinct assets:

1. Passport Photograph (20 KB &ndash; 50 KB)
2. Signature Scan (10 KB &ndash; 20 KB)
3. Left Thumb Impression (LTI) (20 KB &ndash; 50 KB)
4. Handwritten Declaration (50 KB &ndash; 100 KB)

In this article, we break down each requirement so your application sails through validation without delay.

---

## 1. Photograph Specifications (200 &times; 230 Pixels)

- **Dimensions:** 200 &times; 230 pixels (preferred) or 4.5 cm &times; 3.5 cm.
- **File Size:** Between **20 KB and 50 KB**.
- **Background:** Light-colored, preferably white background.
- **Date:** Taken recently, preferably within the last 6 months.
- **Important:** Ensure the candidate looks straight into the camera with a relaxed face. Red-eye reflections or shadows will lead to disqualification.

---

## 2. Signature Specifications (140 &times; 60 Pixels)

- **Dimensions:** 140 &times; 60 pixels.
- **File Size:** Between **10 KB and 20 KB**.
- **Important Prohibition:** **Never sign in capital/block letters**. IBPS guidelines state that signatures in all capital letters will not be accepted.
- **Ink:** Black ink pen on white paper is mandatory for banking exams.

---

## 3. Left Thumb Impression (LTI) Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 240 &times; 240 pixels (1:1 square aspect ratio) or 3 cm &times; 3 cm.
- **File Size:** 20 KB to 50 KB.
- **Ink:** Blue or black stamp pad ink on white paper.
- **Clarity:** Ensure ridges and lines are distinct without smudging.

---
