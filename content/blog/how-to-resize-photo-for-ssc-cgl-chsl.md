# How to Resize Photo for SSC CGL, CHSL & MTS (20KB - 50KB Official Guide)

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) conducts recruitment examinations for thousands of central government vacancies each year, including CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, and Stenographer. During the online application process, one of the most frustrating obstacles candidates face is the strict photo upload specification.

Automated validation engines on the SSC portal (ssc.gov.in) immediately reject images that exceed 50 KB or fail to match the required 3.5 cm by 4.5 cm aspect ratio. In this guide, you will learn the exact official requirements and how to resize your photograph in under 30 seconds for free.

---

## 1. Official SSC Photograph Requirements

According to the latest Staff Selection Commission recruitment notifications:

- **Dimensions:** 3.5 cm (width) &times; 4.5 cm (height)
- **Pixel Resolution:** Approximately 132 &times; 170 pixels (at 96 DPI standard)
- **File Size Window:** Strictly between **20 KB** and **50 KB**
- **File Format:** JPEG or JPG format only
- **Background:** Clean, plain light or white background
- **Facial Coverage:** Frontal view with 80% facial coverage from chin to forehead

---

## 2. Common Mistakes That Cause SSC Application Rejection

1. **Distorted Aspect Ratio:** Compressing a widescreen photo into a tall portrait squashes facial features. Reviewers reject stretched or blurred images.
2. **Uploading Greater than 50 KB:** The upload script will display a red error message: *"Image size exceeds 50 KB"*.
3. **Wearing Glasses or Caps:** Caps, hats, goggles, and dark spectacles are strictly banned in SSC applications. Normal prescription glasses should not have glare reflecting into the camera lens.
4. **Poor Lighting or Shadows:** Flash shadows cast across the background or uneven lighting across the cheeks lead to automated disqualification.
